About Pulita

Chinese engineering. Nigerian operational excellence.

A global original equipment manufacturer of gas-powered energy systems, with a 30,000 m² factory in China and our own teams on the ground in Nigeria.

Our story

From Weifang to West Africa.

Pulita is a global Original Equipment Manufacturer headquartered in China, with operational offices in Nigeria. We specialise in advanced gas-powered energy solutions for industrial, commercial and institutional clients.

Our factory sits in the Sino-foreign Cooperation Industrial Park of the Binhai Economic Development Zone in Weifang. It covers more than 30,000 m², including 10,000 m² of production workshops, with a designed capacity of 4,000 generating sets a year from 10 kVA to 4,000 kVA.

Raw material purchasing, drawing design, production and manufacturing all follow the ISO 9001 quality system, and our products carry EU CE certification. We back them with more than ten years of production experience and a reliable international supply chain.

In Nigeria, where diesel costs are crippling businesses, we saw the chance to do more than export generators. So we built a local presence: offices in Lagos and Abuja, trained engineers and a spare-parts warehouse that keeps customers running.

Quality control

Defects don’t move forward.

Strict standards for product testing and process inspection cover every step, from materials arriving in the workshop to delivery on your site.

Raw materials

Incoming materials and components are inspected and traced before they enter the workshop.

Drawing design

Engineering designs are reviewed against the customer’s load, fuel and site conditions.

Production

Sets are assembled in a 10,000 m² production workshop under ISO 9001 process control.

Process inspection

Professional inspectors check every stage. A defective set cannot move to the next step until the problem is fixed.

ISO 8528 testing

Finished sets are load-tested in Pulita’s own testing centre to ISO 8528 and any special industry or regional requirements.

Delivery & commissioning

Units ship with documentation, then our Nigerian engineers install, commission and train your team on site.

Advanced testing centre

Pulita has built its own testing centre for new product design and delivery inspection. Tests follow the ISO 8528 standard plus any performance requirements specific to your industry or region.
