600–7,000 m³/h

Standard CNG daughter station EPC & petrol station conversion

Turnkey CNG daughter stations supporting gas flow rates of 600–7,000 m³/h. We also convert old petrol stations into CNG/LNG refuelling stations, so existing forecourts can start selling gas quickly.

2–10 MMSCF/d

EPC services for CNG mother stations

Pipeline-fed CNG mother stations with capacities from 2 to 10 MMSCF/d. Gas is treated, metered and compressed to 250 bar, then loaded into tube trailers that supply daughter stations, MRUs and gas-powered sites.

20–60 tons

L-CNG refuelling stations

L-CNG stations store LNG on site and convert it to CNG for dispensing, bringing natural gas to locations far from any pipeline. Station capacities range from 20 to 60 tons.
