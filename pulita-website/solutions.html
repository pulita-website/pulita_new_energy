Solutions

From gas source to kilowatt.

Pulita is more than a generator manufacturer. We design, supply and support the entire chain: processing, compression, storage, refuelling and power generation.

The full ecosystem

One partner for the whole gas-to-power chain.

Tap each step to see what Pulita supplies at that stage.

1Step 1 of 5

Gas processing

Raw gas from the pipeline, oil field (flare gas), digester (biogas) or landfill is filtered, dried and purified to engine and CNG specification.

Gas processing equipment →Metering & analysis →

2Step 2 of 5

CNG compression

Mother stations and compressor units raise natural gas to up to 250 bar, turning it into CNG ready to store, transport and dispense.

CNG mother stations →CNG compressor units →

3Step 3 of 5

Storage & transportation

Cylinder cascades (100–7,000 scm) and tube skid trailers (6,000–9,260 scm) form a virtual pipeline, carrying gas to sites the pipeline doesn’t reach. LNG storage is available for larger projects.

Cylinder cascades →Tube skid trailers →

4Step 4 of 5

Refuelling solutions

Daughter stations and mobile refuelling units (MRUs) dispense CNG to cars, buses and trucks. Our vehicle conversion kits put fleets on gas.

CNG daughter stations & MRUs →Abuja MRU project →

5Step 5 of 5

Power generation

Pulita gas generators, fed through a PRMS, turn the gas into reliable electricity for hospitals, factories, estates, telecom sites and stations.

Gas generators →PRMS / DRS skids →

Gas refilling & refuelling solutions

MRU & Kubwa refilling station, Abuja.

In Kubwa, Abuja, Pulita supplied the compressors and a mobile refuelling unit (MRU) for the refilling station. It now refills CNG vehicles, buses and trucks, and supplies gas to power generators.

  • Containerized MRU with compressor, storage cascade and dispenser
  • Fast deployment on site, with no major civil works
  • Serves vehicles, buses, trucks and gas generators
