LPG / CNG vs diesel

Diesel is draining your margins.

The high cost of diesel means high electricity costs. An independent CNG or LPG power plant can cut that cost by 54–77% and quickly pay back the investment in gas infrastructure.

  • Nigeria has abundant natural gas reserves and an expanding pipeline network.
  • LPG is easy to store and widely distributed, from cylinders to bulk tanks.
