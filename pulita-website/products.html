01Primary product

Gas generators

One generator. Five gases.

Pulita gas generator sets run from 20 to 4,000 kVA on the gas available at your site. Choose your fuel and we engineer the genset, gas train and controls around it.

LPG

Liquefied petroleum gas

Widely distributed across Nigeria in cylinders and bulk tanks. Easy to store, ideal where there is no pipeline.

CNG / Natural gas

Pipeline gas or compressed natural gas

Nigeria’s abundant natural gas, by pipeline or delivered as CNG. The lowest running cost, saving up to 77%.

Flare gas

Associated gas from oil fields

Gas that would otherwise be flared becomes electricity, turning waste and emissions into revenue.

Biogas

Farms, abattoirs, food & wastewater

Renewable gas from organic waste powers the site that produces it, with gas cleaning included.

Landfill gas

Methane captured at landfills

Captured landfill methane fuels generators, cutting greenhouse emissions while producing power.

Flare gas, biogas and landfill gas usually need treatment before use. See gas processing equipment.

Generator range

LPG

LPG generator sets

  • More cost-effective: lean combustion yields up to 5 kWh from 1 kg of LPG.
  • Safer: precise ignition, flame arresters and explosion-proof valves.
  • More convenient: abundant refuelling, cylinder swap, one-button start.
  • Containerized options: units can be synchronised for varying loads.
CNG

Natural gas generator sets

  • More efficient: peak generator efficiency of up to 44%.
  • More durable: 24-hour duty, 15-year design life, 30,000-hour major service interval.
  • Flexible fuel: pipeline gas, CNG, biogas, flare and landfill gas versions.
  • Complete system: supplied with cascades, PRMS and gas processing as needed.

Technical data

LPG/CNG generator set specifications.

Genset at 50 Hz, 400/230 V, 1,500 rpm, 3P4W. Gas engine: in-line, four-stroke, electronic ignition, water-cooled. Brushless alternator with AVR. Larger and custom plants up to 4,000 kVA on request.

Other core products

PRMS & DRS skids.

Our pressure regulating and metering stations (PRMS) step high-pressure CNG down to engine pressure, with heating and metering built in. District regulating stations (DRS) do the same for pipeline distribution networks.

  • Multi-stage pressure regulation with heating
  • Flow metering for accurate cost control
  • Safety shut-off and relief valves
  • Weatherproof, lockable skid enclosure

Other core products

Gas processing equipment.

Raw gas is rarely engine-ready. Our processing skids clean, dry and condition pipeline gas, associated (flare) gas, biogas and landfill gas, protecting your compressors and generators.
