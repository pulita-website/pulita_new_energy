01Power generation · primary product

Gas generators

One generator. Five gases.

Pulita gas generator sets run from 20 to 4,000 kVA on the gas available at your site. Choose your fuel and we engineer the genset, gas train and controls around it.

LPG

Liquefied petroleum gas

Widely distributed across Nigeria in cylinders and bulk tanks. Easy to store, ideal where there is no pipeline.

CNG / Natural gas

Pipeline gas or compressed natural gas

Nigeria’s abundant natural gas, by pipeline or delivered as CNG. The lowest running cost, saving up to 77%.

Flare gas

Associated gas from oil fields

Gas that would otherwise be flared becomes electricity, turning waste and emissions into revenue.

Biogas

Farms, abattoirs, food & wastewater

Renewable gas from organic waste powers the site that produces it, with gas cleaning included.

Landfill gas

Methane captured at landfills

Captured landfill methane fuels generators, cutting greenhouse emissions while producing power.

Flare gas, biogas and landfill gas usually need treatment before use. See gas processing equipment.

04Regulation & processing

Regulation & processing

Safe, clean, engine-ready gas.

PRMS/DRS skids step pressure down and meter flow. Processing skids clean and dry raw pipeline gas, flare gas, biogas and landfill gas.

PRMS & DRS skids

  • Multi-stage pressure regulation with heating
  • Flow metering for accurate cost control
  • Safety shut-off and relief valves in a lockable enclosure
  • PLT-600NG PRMS covered by our SONCAP product certificate

Gas processing equipment

  • Filtration & separation: removes liquids, dust and particles
  • Dehydration: dries gas to CNG specification
  • Purification: H₂S and siloxane removal for biogas and landfill gas
  • Metering & analysis: flow and gas-quality measurement
