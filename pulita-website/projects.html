Case study

Burger King Abeokuta: a green restaurant with zero diesel.

How an 80 kVA Pulita LPG generator helped Burger King Nigeria open its first green restaurant in Abeokuta, Ogun State.

80 kVAPulita LPG generator set
0 Ldiesel used on site
~67%lower fuel cost vs diesel*

The challenge

Burger King Nigeria wanted its Abeokuta outlet to set a new standard for sustainable restaurant design. The restaurant had to run kitchens, cold rooms and air-conditioning every day without the diesel generators that power most Nigerian businesses, and without the cost, fumes and noise that come with them.

The solution

Pulita supplied an 80 kVA LPG generator set sized for the restaurant’s full load. Our Nigerian engineers installed and commissioned it on site, connecting it to the LPG supply and setting it up for reliable daily operation. LPG is widely available across Ogun State, so refuelling is simple, and lean-combustion technology gets more electricity from every kilogram of gas.

The result

At the launch, Burger King Nigeria described the Abeokuta outlet as its first green restaurant: built from recycled shipping containers, powered by a gas generator, cooled by gas air-conditioning and running with zero diesel. The site became the chain’s 23rd outlet in Nigeria and a visible showcase of what gas power can do for commercial operators.

*Estimate from Pulita’s LPG vs diesel reference data (100 kW, ₦750/kg LPG vs ₦2,000/L diesel). Actual savings depend on load and local fuel prices.
